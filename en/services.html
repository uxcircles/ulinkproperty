Services and fees

Four services, priced as they are on our current website.

Viewing reportHK$450no deposit, pay after the viewing

Viewing service

Send us the link to a property you like. We book the viewing with the agent, film it and send you a written report.

Outside

  • The house front and the street
  • The area around the property
  • Anything that affects buying or letting, such as empty houses or how lived-in the street is

Inside

  • Video of every room and the back garden
  • Electricity, water and gas meters, and the pipes
  • Visible leaks or structural problems, photographed close up, with a repair estimate
  • Parking: garage or space outside
  • Our conclusion: is the price fair, is it worth buying
Buying service1%of the price, paid on completion

Buying, start to finish

We learn what you need, then recommend and analyse properties, pros and cons both.

  1. Viewing
  2. Offer
  3. Talking to the agent
  4. Instructing a solicitor
  5. Paperwork and completion
  6. Collecting the keys
Tenant find1 monthof rent
Management12%of the rent

Letting and management

Tenant find includes

  • Rental assessment: what rent the market will pay
  • Photos inside and out, edited
  • Description, online listing and advertising
  • Viewings with interested tenants
  • Tenancy agreement
  • Deposit registered with a government-approved scheme

Tenant credit and background checks are charged per check by the referencing company.

Management includes

  • Handling the tenancy and contact with tenants
  • Arranging repairs and renovation
  • Claiming from the deposit for any damage when the tenant leaves
Airbnb15% + VATof bookings, plus a cleaning fee

Airbnb management

Listing, pricing, cleaning, check-in, guest vetting and 24/7 replies. The cleaning fee depends on how many guests stayed; guests pay most of it.
